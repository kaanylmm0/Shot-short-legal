Privacy Policy for ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer

Effective Date: October 4, 2026
Last Updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer (“ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer,” “the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer mobile application.

Privacy-first design: ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer is designed so that screenshot discovery, OCR, categorization, duplicate detection, similarity detection, QR detection, search indexing, and cleanup analysis are performed locally on your device whenever technically possible.

1. Overview

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer is a screenshot organization and cleanup utility. Features may include finding screenshots stored on your device, organizing screenshots into categories, on-device OCR, text search, duplicate and similar screenshot detection, age-based cleanup, cleanup recommendations, storage statistics, and user-initiated deletion.

2. Photo Library Access

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer requires permission to access photos stored in your device’s photo library in order to identify and organize screenshots. Depending on your operating system, you may grant full access, limited access, selected-photo access, or no access. You may change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer cannot access photos that your operating system does not make available to the App.

3. Your Photos and Screenshots

We do not manually view your photo library

The developer of ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer does not manually view, monitor, review, or browse the photos or screenshots stored on your device through the App.

Photos are not uploaded to our servers

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer does not operate a cloud backend for storing or remotely analyzing your screenshots. Your screenshots are not uploaded to a ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer-owned server for OCR, categorization, duplicate detection, search, or cleanup analysis.

Local processing

Current ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer functionality is designed to process screenshot content locally on your device. This includes screenshot detection, metadata processing, OCR, screenshot categorization, duplicate detection, similarity detection, QR code detection, search indexing, and cleanup analysis.

4. OCR and Screenshot Content

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may analyze visible text inside screenshots to enable search and automatic categorization. Recognized text may include names, messages, prices, product names, addresses, dates, notes, website text, social-media text, receipt information, or other content visible in the screenshot.

Under the current design, OCR processing occurs locally on your device. We do not receive or manually review OCR text extracted from your screenshots.

5. Local Database and Cache

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may store local analysis data on your device, including screenshot identifiers, file metadata, modification dates, OCR results, category results, duplicate-analysis results, similarity-analysis results, cleanup information, and application preferences.

This local cache helps avoid analyzing the same screenshot repeatedly and improves performance. Deleting ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may remove local analysis data according to your operating system’s application-deletion behavior. Deleting the App does not necessarily delete original screenshots stored in your photo library.

6. Screenshot Deletion

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer allows users to select screenshots for deletion. ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer does not intentionally delete screenshots merely because they belong to a category or are detected as similar or duplicated.

Deletion is initiated by the user and may require additional confirmation from the operating system. Depending on the platform, a deleted item may be moved to a “Recently Deleted,” “Trash,” “Bin,” or similar location before permanent deletion.

We recommend carefully reviewing selected screenshots before confirming deletion.

7. Automatic Categorization

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may automatically categorize screenshots into categories such as Shopping, Receipts, Messages, Social Media, Notes, QR Codes, Memes, and Other.

Automated categorization may be inaccurate. A screenshot may be assigned to the wrong category, remain uncategorized, be incorrectly identified as similar, or contain text that OCR does not recognize correctly.

Categories and recommendations are organizational suggestions only and should not be treated as factual determinations.

8. Duplicate and Similar Screenshot Detection

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may use local file hashes, perceptual hashes, thumbnails, image metadata, and similar techniques to identify exact duplicates or visually similar screenshots.

No automated similarity system is perfect. Two different but important screenshots may occasionally be identified as similar. Any “Keep,” “Best,” or similar recommendation is only a software-generated suggestion.

9. Purchases and Subscriptions

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may offer Free, Monthly Pro, Yearly Pro, and Lifetime Pro access. Purchases are processed by the applicable platform provider such as Apple App Store or Google Play.

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer does not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

10. RevenueCat

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may use RevenueCat to manage and verify purchases, subscriptions, entitlements, and restore-purchase functionality. RevenueCat may process an anonymous App User ID or similar identifier, product identifiers, purchase status, subscription status, expiration dates, entitlement status, and store transaction information necessary to provide purchase-management functionality.

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer does not intentionally send your screenshot images or OCR content to RevenueCat.

11. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Through Our Own Backend

  • Your screenshot images
  • Photos from your photo library
  • OCR text extracted from screenshots
  • Your contacts
  • Precise GPS location
  • Health data
  • Private messages from other apps
  • Browsing history outside ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer
  • Complete payment card details

12. Analytics and Advertising

The current version of ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer does not use a ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer-operated analytics backend to collect screenshot content.

If analytics, crash reporting, or advertising technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy and applicable store privacy disclosures will be updated as required. Third-party SDKs may have their own privacy practices.

13. Internet Connection

Many organization and analysis features are designed to work without an internet connection. Internet access may still be required for purchases, purchase restoration, retrieving current product prices, verifying subscription status, opening legal/support pages, and communicating with app marketplaces or purchase-management services.

14. Data Retention

Local screenshot-analysis data may remain on your device until the related screenshot is changed or deleted, the App invalidates the cache, you clear application data, you uninstall the App, or your operating system removes the App’s data.

Purchase information handled by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or another purchase provider may be retained according to those providers’ own policies and legal obligations.

15. Data Security

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer is designed to minimize transmission of personal screenshot content. However, no software, device, storage system, or security mechanism can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, device passcode, Apple ID or Google account, and device backups.

16. Children’s Privacy

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly operate a system designed to collect children’s personal information through screenshot uploads to our servers.

17. Your Choices

You can control ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer’s access through your device settings. Depending on your operating system, you may revoke photo access, switch to limited access, modify selected photos, manage subscriptions, or uninstall ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer.

18. Third-Party Services

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may rely on operating-system services and third-party providers, including Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and local device frameworks. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

19. International Users

ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer may be available in multiple countries. Different privacy laws may apply depending on your location. To the extent applicable law gives you rights concerning personal data processed by us, you may contact us using the details below.

Because most ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer screenshot analysis occurs locally on your device, we may not possess screenshot-related information that you request from us.

20. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ShotMate: Screenshot Organizer features change, new technologies are added, third-party services change, or legal requirements evolve. The “Last Updated” date above will reflect the latest revision.
